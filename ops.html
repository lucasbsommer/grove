The Journey

Building rural vineyard infrastructure in Washington County is a multi-year regulatory marathon. Here's the real timeline — from buying the land to the permits finally issuing — documented in public.
Jul 11, 2022
Acquisition — purchased for $420,000. Grove Vineyard, LLC bought the 26.61-acre parcel on NW Timmerman Rd (rec. #2022045801), financed via Cetan Funds + an FSA farm loan. Prior sales: $150K (1995), $113K (1994). Land survey commissioned in Forest Grove that fall.
Jun 2023
Grove Vineyard, LLC formed. Operating agreement executed; Value-Added Producer Grant explored.
Aug 2023
First approach to the county. Removal/Fill permit inquiry with Washington County for the existing access + creek culvert.
2023 — Setback
Code-enforcement violation opened (ENFPDS23-00054). The county flags the existing private road + culvert as unpermitted work. The clock — and the fines exposure — start.
Mar 2024
Grading-permit effort begins. Sommer / Grove Vineyard grading permit thread opens with the county.
Jun–Oct 2024
Team assembled. Rapid Soil Solutions engaged for geotech (Jun); Emerio Design engaged for civil + planning (Oct).
Dec 2024 — The gauntlet
Forced into a Type II Drainage Hazard Analysis. Because a stream crosses the parcel, the county requires a full DHA permit. Application submitted Dec 20 — then a county incompleteness letter sends it back for a revise-and-resubmit.
Mar 2025 — Win
DHA approved with conditions (Case L2500008-DHA). The drainage-hazard department is cleared. FEMA confirmed the parcel sits outside all flood zones. The conditions roll forward into the grading permit.
Aug–Sep 2025
Culvert engineered + documented. Rapid Soil (Mia Mahedy, PE) issues the stamped culvert field memo for the building permit.
Jan 13 2026 — Win
Permits ISSUED. Grading permit GRAD-2505132 and culvert permit BLDG-2514996 both issued and paid. Cleared to build.
Now — 2026
Site prep & the pre-grading meeting. Clearing the corridor, then the mandatory pre-grading meeting → grading → final inspection, which closes the violation. Deadline ~July 12, 2026.
