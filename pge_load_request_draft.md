Dear Service and Design Team,

Grove Vineyard, LLC owns the two parcels above, which front OR-6 (Wilson River Highway) west of Forest Grove. We are evaluating a commercial DC fast-charging site on the frontage and are requesting a service availability review and preliminary load study.

**What we are planning**

- A DC fast-charging station of up to eight 400 to 500 kW charging posts (Tesla V4 Supercharger class or equivalent), built in phases.
- Phase 1: four posts, roughly 1,000 kVA of demand.
- Build-out: up to eight posts, roughly 2,000 kVA (about 2 MW) of demand.
- Service: 480V three-phase, metered commercial, with on-site transformer(s) and switchgear sized to the above.

**What we already see at the frontage**

There is an existing three-phase primary distribution line on the poles along the OR-6 frontage, at or adjacent to our property line. We would prefer to interconnect to that frontage line directly rather than extend single-phase service from a side road. We understand final capacity depends on your feeder and substation analysis.

**What we are asking you to confirm**

1. Available capacity on the frontage feeder for a new 1,000 kVA load (Phase 1) and a 2,000 kVA load (full build-out).
2. The likely point of interconnection and whether a line extension, reconductoring, new transformer bank, or substation work would be required.
3. Any system-upgrade or make-ready costs, and whether this qualifies for a PGE make-ready or commercial EV charging program.
4. Budgetary cost estimate and a rough timeline from application to energization.
5. The application and engineering-study steps you need from us to start, including any deposit or load data forms.

**Site context that may help your review**

- The parcels take access from the county road, and a Washington County Type II commercial land-use approval with permitted road and access is already in place.
- The site is vacant and graded for access, so there are no demolition or existing-service complications.

Please let us know the next step and any information you need from us. We can provide a site plan, preliminary one-line, and equipment cut sheets on request.
